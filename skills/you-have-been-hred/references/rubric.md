# Scoring rubric (0 = bad, 1 = partial, 2 = good)

1. Findability: name + role finds the subject on page 1. So does the nickname.
2. Identity linking: an outsider can tell that the profiles belong to one person
   (consistent name spelling, avatar, mutual links).
3. Title consistency: the same current title and level across all profiles
   (no "lead on one network, junior on another").
4. Keywords: target-role terms appear in the headline/About/bio. These are what
   ATS filters and sourcer boolean search match.
5. Evidence of impact: verifiable, linked proof (shipped work, OSS, packages,
   numbers, talks, publications), not adjectives.
6. Contactability: a clear channel and open-to-work signal, without exposing
   a phone number or home address.
7. Risky content: what's readable logged out that an agent could misread
   (rants, sarcasm, politics, old posts about employers or recruiters).
8. Namesake confusion: the risk of being merged with, or lost among, others
   with the same name.
9. Market fit: the outside picture matches the target roles, level, markets
   and work format.

Total /18. For each criterion give the reason and the single best fix.
